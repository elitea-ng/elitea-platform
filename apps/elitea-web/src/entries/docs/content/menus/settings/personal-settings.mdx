Personal settings apply to you across every project — they never affect what your teammates see.

## Profile

Settings › Profile shows your full name, email, user ID, and last login, and is where you sign out of Elitea.

<Screenshot id="settings-profile" alt="The personal Profile page" />

## Preferences

Settings › Preferences covers three areas:

- **General** — your theme: Dark, Light, or System.
- **Voice personalization** — your default text-to-speech voice, speed, and volume, with a preview button. Uses your project's configured server-side voice model when available, otherwise your browser's built-in voices.
- **Sound notifications** — whether Elitea plays a completion sound when a task finishes, and at what volume.

<Screenshot id="settings-preferences" alt="Personal Preferences with theme and voice settings" />

## AI Personality

Settings › AI Personality sets the default communication style and any standing instructions applied to every new conversation you start.

<Screenshot id="settings-ai-personality" alt="AI Personality persona and instructions editor" />

- **Default Personality** — pick from Generic, QA, Nerdy, Quirky, Cynical, None (no personality overlay), or Bare (no Elitea identity at all, only your instructions).
- **User Instructions** — free-text guidance (communication preferences, technical requirements, output format) that applies automatically to new conversations, agents, and pipelines. Settings save automatically as you edit; only new conversations pick up a change — existing ones keep what they started with.

<Tip>
Start with the default persona and no instructions, use it for a few conversations, then adjust one setting at a time based on what you notice.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A change does not seem to apply">Confirm you are testing in a brand-new conversation — existing conversations keep their original settings.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Personality change has no visible effect">Personality shapes tone and approach, not factual correctness — it is most visible in open-ended or creative requests, not factual lookups.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
