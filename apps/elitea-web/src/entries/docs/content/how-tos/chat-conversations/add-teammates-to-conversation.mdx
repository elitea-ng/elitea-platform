---
title: "Add teammates to a conversation"
description: "Invite other project members into a conversation, mention them, and manage who's participating."
---

<Note>
Adding human teammates to a conversation only works in team projects, not in your personal project.
</Note>

## Add a teammate

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the add-participants control">
In the **Participants** panel, click the add-participants icon.
</Step>
<Step title="Search and select">
A dialog opens with a search box listing project members. Search by name and select one or more people.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Click **Add**. The people you selected appear in the Users section of the Participants panel and receive a notification that they've been added.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot id="chat-add-participant-dialog" alt="Add-participant dialog listing project members with a search box" />

## Mention a teammate

Type `@` in the message box to open a suggestion list of everyone in the conversation, then pick a name (or select **Everyone**, shown once the conversation has two or more human participants, to notify the whole group). A mention only directs the message at that person — agents and pipelines in the conversation don't treat it as an instruction to act.

## Remove a teammate

Open the Users list in the Participants panel, hover over the person's name, and click the delete icon that appears, then confirm. You can't remove yourself this way.

## Who can see the conversation

Adding a teammate only shares the conversation with the people you explicitly add — it does not make the conversation visible to the rest of the project unless someone also marks it public (see [Use chat](/how-tos/chat-conversations/how-to-use-chat-functionality) for visibility rules). Removing a teammate stops their access going forward but doesn't retroactively hide messages they've already read.

This is different from adding an agent, pipeline, or toolkit as a participant — those are attached from the composer's **+** menu, not this dialog, and they don't count toward "teammates." See [Use chat](/how-tos/chat-conversations/how-to-use-chat-functionality#attach-participants) for that flow.
