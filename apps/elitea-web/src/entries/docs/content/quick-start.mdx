This walks through the shortest path from an invite to a working
conversation.

<Note>
You need an invite or an account created by your organization's admin —
Elitea does not have open self-signup.
</Note>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to your organization's Elitea URL and choose your identity provider
    on the sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your personal project">
    Elitea provisions a personal project for every user on first sign-in —
    it is where your own chats, agents, and credentials live before you
    share anything with a team project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message">
    Open **Chat** and type a message. The default model responds using
    whatever provider your project has configured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose how you connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Browser">
    The web app at your organization's Elitea URL. This is what most people
    mean by "Elitea" day to day.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    The same platform over its `/llm` OpenAI-compatible endpoint, for
    scripts and integrations that need programmatic access rather than a
    chat window.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
An API key inherits the permissions of the project it was issued under.
Treat it like a password: it is not tied to your browser session and does
not expire when you sign out.
</Warning>

## Frequently asked

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my own model provider?">
  Yes, provided a project admin has added your provider's credentials under
  that project's settings. See [Toolkits overview](/toolkits-overview) for
  how external connections are configured more generally.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to a shared conversation link?">
  A share link opens the conversation read-only for anyone who has it, with
  no sign-in required — see [Chat basics](/chat-basics#sharing-a-conversation).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
