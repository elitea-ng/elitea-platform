The first time you sign in, Elitea takes you to the onboarding screen before dropping you into Chat. Use it, and this page, to get oriented quickly.

## Find your way around

- The **Elitea icon** at the top of the sidebar opens the full menu: Chat, Agents, Pipelines, Skills, Toolkits, Applications, Credentials, Artifacts, and Settings.
- The **Project Selector** at the top of the sidebar switches between your personal project and any team projects you belong to. Start experimenting in your personal project before sharing work with a team.
- The **socket connection indicator** on the Elitea logo shows whether your browser has a live connection to the server (green) or has lost it (red).
- A **?** button near the bottom of the sidebar opens the [Help Center](/menus/help-and-support) with documentation, release notes, and tutorials configured by your administrator.

## Your first hour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    Chat is where everything comes together. Start a new conversation and send a message directly to a model — no setup required for a plain conversation. See [Chat](/menus/chat).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a toolkit">
    In the Participants panel, add a toolkit (for example GitHub or Jira) so the conversation can act on an external service. Create the toolkit and its credential right from the chat editor. See [Toolkits](/menus/toolkits) and [Credentials](/menus/credentials).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Add an Agent participant and use its inline editor to write instructions, pick a model, and attach the toolkit you just created. See [Agents](/menus/agents).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Mention the agent with `#` in the chat input, activate it, and send a message. Iterate on the instructions until the responses look right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a pipeline when you need multiple steps">
    Once a task needs more than one agent or a decision point, move it into a [pipeline](/menus/pipelines).
  </Step>
</Steps>
