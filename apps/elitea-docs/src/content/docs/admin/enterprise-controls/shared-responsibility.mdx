---
title: "Shared responsibility"
description: "What Elitea enforces, what it records, and what deliberately stays with your organisation's own systems — and how the two connect."
---

Elitea enforces controls at the points it owns: model traffic, tool calls, credentials, runs and configuration. It records what happened as evidence, and exports that evidence. It does **not** try to replace the systems a regulated organisation already runs for reviews, records, identity governance or security monitoring. Rebuilding those inside an AI platform would produce a weaker copy that your auditors would then have to assess separately.

The table shows where the line sits and how Elitea integrates across it.

| Responsibility | Lives in | What Elitea provides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture, legal and compliance, and model-risk reviews | Your GRC or ticketing system | Typed attestations on the agent or release, required by risk tier before approval, and writable over the API ([attestations](/admin/enterprise-controls/agent-lifecycle#attestations)) |
| Data owner approval and data accuracy | The data owner and your data catalogue | Credential use grants with an approver other than the proposer ([credential governance](/deployment/credential-governance)); `data_owner` and `data_accuracy` attestations with an expiry ([attestations](/admin/enterprise-controls/agent-lifecycle#attestations)) |
| Immutable storage of record (WORM) | Object storage with object lock, or your SIEM's archive tier | Hash-chained logs that make tampering detectable, exported in one versioned schema ([audit event schema](/reference/audit-event-schema)). Elitea does not claim WORM on its own database. |
| Retention schedule | Your records-management policy | Per-class retention limits and legal hold that enforce the values you set ([audit evidence and retention](/admin/enterprise-controls/audit-evidence-and-retention)) |
| Security monitoring, correlation and anomaly detection | Your SIEM or SOC | The evidence export (including every agent run's [step records](/admin/enterprise-controls/audit-evidence-and-retention#run-step-records)), Prometheus metrics, alert rules for platform health, a per-agent Grafana dashboard, and a rule-based [agent circuit breaker](/admin/enterprise-controls/runtime-safety#agent-circuit-breaker) that stops a failing agent. |
| Paging and on-call | Your incident-management tool | Alerts (Prometheus rules for breaker trips, breaker storms and active suspensions; a platform stop is critical), the automatic stop of the [agent circuit breaker](/admin/enterprise-controls/runtime-safety#agent-circuit-breaker), and the [emergency stop](/deployment/emergency-stop-runbook) that on-call staff use. Elitea does not page a person: route the alerts to your on-call. |
| Rotation of externally issued secrets | Your secret manager (Vault or OpenBao) or the issuing system | The secret-store integration, expiry on tokens and grants, and revocation ([secrets](/admin/secrets)) |
| Content classifiers and harm taxonomy | Your trust-and-safety or compliance function | The gateway enforcement point and adapters to the classifier you choose (planned, [issue 1500](https://github.com/elitea-ng/elitea-platform/issues/1500)) |
| Statistical drift on production traffic | Your ML-monitoring tool | The evidence stream as input, and an API to mark an agent as due for recertification (planned) |
| Bias and fairness methodology | Your model-risk function | An evaluation harness that runs any suite and scoring dimension you define |
| Business thresholds (amounts, novelty, risk appetite) | Your risk owners' written policy | Enforcement of the thresholds and tool categories you configure |
| Sign-in frequency and multi-factor authentication | Your identity provider | Session maximums and an audit event for every session start, expiry, sign-out and revocation ([session policy](/admin/authentication#session-policy)) |
| Access reviews and entitlement campaigns | Your identity governance (IGA) tool | The [entitlement export](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust#entitlement-export-for-access-reviews) as CSV and SCIM, the [credential inventory](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust#credential-inventory) and the [permission trimming report](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust#permission-trimming) |
| Architecture review of new connectors | Your architecture or security review board | [Connector approval](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust#connector-approval) with a second approver and an optional mandatory attestation |
| Network perimeter | Your network team | Egress guard, the [worker egress allowlist](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust) and the record of hosts each run reached |
| Trust in remote agents and their issuers | Your security architecture and the owners of the remote systems | [Signed assertions](/admin/enterprise-controls/least-privilege-and-trust#remote-agents) on calls to remote MCP servers, a list of trusted issuers that an administrator keeps, and refusal of replayed or expired assertions. You decide which issuers to trust and verify assertions on your own servers. |

<Tip>
In a risk assessment, cite this page alongside the [control catalogue](/admin/enterprise-controls/overview). A control your organisation provides is a dependency to record, not a gap.
</Tip>
