Elitea delivers real-time notifications over a live connection for events like publish decisions, mentions, budget thresholds, and expiring tokens.

<Screenshot id="settings-notifications" alt="Notifications Center with the notification table" />

## Quick popover

Select the bell icon in the sidebar's header for a fast, unread-only view: up to 5 items, with **Mark as read** on hover and **Mark all as read** / **View all** in the footer. A dot on the bell means there is something unread. In the desktop app, the bell sits in the sidebar's title bar, beside **Hide sidebar**.

## System notifications (desktop app)

While the desktop app's window is in the background, a new notification also shows as a system notification: its kind as the title and its message as the text. Nothing shows while you are working in the app — the bell has it. The app's Dock icon badge counts unread notifications together with approvals waiting for you. Select a system notification to bring the app to the front.

To stop system notifications, turn off **Show system notifications** at the top of Settings › Notifications (desktop app only). The bell and Notifications Center are not affected. Your operating system's own notification settings for Elitea also apply.

## Notifications Center

Settings › Notifications shows every notification, read and unread, with search by message text, sortable **Type** and **Date & Time** columns, and bulk **Mark as read**, **Mark as unread**, and **Delete** actions.

## What triggers a notification

| Event | What it means |
|---|---|
| Publish approved / rejected | Your published agent or skill version was reviewed. |
| Mentioned or added to a conversation | A teammate `@mentioned` you or added you to a conversation. |
| Index created, reindexed, or failed | A toolkit index finished or failed. |
| Bucket expiration warning | Files in an artifact bucket will be deleted within 24 hours under its retention policy. |
| Personal access token expiring | A token will stop working within 24 hours. |
| Project or member budget threshold / limit reached | Shared-model spend is near or at its monthly limit. |

Several of these link straight to the relevant page — for example, an expiring-token notification opens Settings › Personal Tokens, and a budget notification opens Settings › Usage.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bell still shows unread after I opened everything">Opening the popover does not mark items read automatically — use **Mark as read** or **Mark all as read** explicitly.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No system notifications in the desktop app">Check that **Show system notifications** is on in Settings › Notifications and that your operating system allows notifications from Elitea. System notifications show only while the app's window is in the background.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A notification's link goes somewhere unexpected">Some links (for example, a mention) open the exact conversation and message; others open a settings page scoped to the relevant project.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
