Catalog is the shared library of published agents and skills, visible across every project you have access to — a discovery surface, not a personal workspace. Reach it from the **Catalog** button near the bottom of the sidebar.

<Screenshot id="catalog-agents" alt="Catalog Agents tab showing published agent cards grouped by category" />

## Agents and Skills tabs

- **Agents** — published agents shown as cards with creator, likes, and a **Start chat** action. Select a card to view its full description, instructions (read-only), welcome message, and conversation starters.
- **Skills** — published skills you can review and attach to one or more of your own agents with **Add** or **Add & Test** (the latter also opens a chat with the agent you attached it to).

Filter either tab by category (Trending, My liked, Development, DevOps, and more) or search by name and description.

## Start a conversation from Catalog

Select **Start chat** on an agent, or one of its conversation starters, to create a new conversation with that agent already active and its participant added.

## What you can and cannot change

Published items are read-only. In a conversation with a published agent you can change the **model**, its **settings** (Creativity or Reasoning Effort, Max Completion Tokens, Steps Limit), and the values of any variables it defines — these changes apply only to your conversation. You cannot edit its name, instructions, welcome message, conversation starters, or attached toolkits; to get an editable copy, [fork](/how-tos/agents-pipelines/forking) it or build your own from the [Agents](/menus/agents) menu.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Expected agent or skill is missing">It may not be published yet, still pending moderation, or filtered out by an active search or category filter.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot start a conversation">Refresh the page and confirm you still have Chat access; if the problem persists, contact your administrator.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
