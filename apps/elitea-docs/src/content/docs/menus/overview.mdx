Every menu in Elitea shares the same shell: a sidebar for navigation, a project selector, and a consistent set of list, search, and organization controls. This page covers what is common; each menu's own page covers what is specific to it.

<Screenshot id="overview-sidebar" alt="Elitea sidebar with the main menu items and project selector" />

## Sidebar

The sidebar is the same in the browser and in the desktop app. From the top:

- **Header** — the Elitea logo, the notification bell, and the button that collapses the sidebar (in the desktop app, the bell sits in the title bar beside **Hide sidebar**).
- **Project selector** — everything below it belongs to the selected project.
- **Chats** — your conversations: **Pinned**, your folders, then the date groups (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Previous 7 days**, …), and an **Archived** row at the end. The Chats caption has buttons to search the chats, create a folder, start a new chat, and show or hide the list. In the browser, its **⋯** menu has a **Local work only** filter.
- **Applications** and **Artifacts**, then **Workshop** — a group you open to reach **Agents**, **Pipelines**, **Skills**, **Toolkits & Indexes**, and **MCPs**.
- **Your account** at the bottom: your picture and name open the [account menu](#account-menu). When the deployment has a support assistant, the **Support Bot** bar is under it.

### Account menu

The account menu is the same in the browser and in the desktop app. Select your picture and name at the bottom of the sidebar, or your picture alone when the sidebar is collapsed. The menu opens upward. From the top:

- Your email address.
- **Settings**, **Usage** (when the deployment tracks costs), **Credentials** (when you may open them), and the **Catalog**.
- **Admin portal**, only when your account holds an administration permission. In the browser it opens the [admin console](/admin/overview) in the same tab; Ctrl-click or ⌘-click opens a new tab. The desktop app opens it in your web browser, where the console uses your browser session.
- **Appearance** (System, Light or Dark) and **Language**.
- **Get help** (the Help Center) and **What's new**. The desktop app also has **Troubleshoot**, and a bug report button beside your name.
- The version line, for example **Version 1.72.3 · Server 1.72**: the version of the app you are using, and the version of the server. Select it to copy it, for example for a support request. A local or development build reads **dev**.
- **Sign out**, always last.

Press Esc to close the menu; the focus goes back to your name.

### Creating from the sidebar

Each place you can add to has a **+** at the end of its row — **New agent**, **New pipeline**, **New skill**, **New toolkit**, **New MCP**, **New application** (opens the catalog), and **New bucket**. The **+** appears when you point at the row or move to it with the keyboard. The **+** in the Chats caption starts a new chat. A **+** is left out when you don't have permission to create that item in the selected project, and in the public project when you have no personal project. Each list page also has its own **New** button.

### Collapsed sidebar

Collapse the sidebar to keep only an icon for each place and a single **+** that starts a new chat. Point at an icon to see its name. The chat list is hidden until you expand the sidebar again. Your picture stays at the bottom and opens the same account menu.

## Project selector

Use the project selector at the top of the sidebar to switch between your **personal project** — visible only to you — and any **team projects** you belong to, where entities are shared and collaboratively managed according to each member's role.

## Common list controls

Most menus (Agents, Pipelines, Skills, Toolkits, Credentials) share the same dashboard pattern:

- **Card view** and **table view**, toggled from the top of the list.
- A **search box** scoped to the current menu — search does not span menus.
- **Tag filters** to narrow the list by category.
- **Pinning** — pin an item to keep it at the top of its list.
- **Sorting** (table view) by name, created date, type, or author.
