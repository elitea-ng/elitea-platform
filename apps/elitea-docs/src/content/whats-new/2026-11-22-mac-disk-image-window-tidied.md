## The Mac disk image window is tidier

Opening the Mac disk image now shows a plain window: the background, the app, the **Applications** folder and the arrow between them, with no toolbar, tab bar or sidebar. The background is lighter, so the names under the two icons are easy to read in light and dark mode.

See [Managed devices](/admin/managed-devices#install-on-a-mac-yourself).
