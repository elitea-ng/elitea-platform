## Sign in with Microsoft from the credential form: Outlook, Teams, Microsoft Graph and Dataverse

The four Microsoft credential types now appear in the **New credential** list on the web. **Save and sign in with Microsoft** saves the credential, opens a Microsoft window and finishes the sign-in by itself; the form then shows who is signed in, the tenant and when the sign-in ends, checks the connection with your own sign-in, and offers **Sign in again** and **Sign out**. If the check finds Microsoft no longer accepts the sign-in, the form asks you to sign in again. Scopes are checkboxes for Outlook and Teams, with what Microsoft does with them explained; **Default app** and **Custom app** are both offered once an administrator registered an app, and only Custom app until then. A person who cannot update credentials sees **Login managed by a project admin**. The desktop app's system-browser sign-in and the mobile app's sign-in follow in the next release; the toolkits that use these credentials follow later. See [Microsoft sign-in](/integrations/third-party-integrations/microsoft-sign-in).
