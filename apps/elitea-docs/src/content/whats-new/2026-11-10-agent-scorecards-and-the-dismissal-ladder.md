## Agent scorecards and the dismissal ladder

Every agent now has a daily scorecard on a new **Workforce** tab: runs and failure rate, budget-cap stops, breaker trips, approval rejections, guardrail hits, spend against the project median, feedback and last use. A person, or a workforce manager agent with the `can_review_agents` capability, can propose **Watch**, **Demote** (taking away exactly what it names), **Suspend**, **Retire** or **Delete**; a person with the new `agents.workforce.manage` permission or the agent's owner decides, after an impact preview. A retired agent leaves conversations, saved teams, nesting and schedules and can be **reinstated** for 30 days. The owner can appeal once to a different manager. Critical-tier agents and agents under legal hold are exempt. **Upgrade every worker before Main.** See [Agent workforce](/how-tos/agents-pipelines/agent-workforce/).
