## Microsoft credentials: Outlook, Teams, Microsoft Graph and Dataverse

The platform now knows four more credential types (hidden from the **New credential** list until their sign-in is ready on web, desktop and mobile). They sign in with your own Microsoft account (Entra delegated OAuth) and are checked by an identity read after you sign in, never by reading your content. Scopes are checkboxes for Outlook and Teams and validated free text for Microsoft Graph; Dataverse is pinned to `*.dynamics.com`. The toolkits and the sign-in window follow in later releases; SharePoint's sign-in is unchanged. See [Microsoft credentials](/menus/credentials#microsoft-credentials-outlook-teams-microsoft-graph-and-dataverse).
