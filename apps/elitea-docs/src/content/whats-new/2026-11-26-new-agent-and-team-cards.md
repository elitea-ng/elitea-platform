## New agent and team cards

The **Agents** list has new cards. Each card shows the agent's own avatar, animated while you point at it or move focus into it. An agent without an avatar shows its initials instead of a placeholder. Under the name you see the persona's role and two lines of the description. The **Draft** or **Published** badge sits next to the name, and a filled pin marks a pinned agent. The creator, at most two tags and when the agent last changed sit in a quiet line at the bottom. **Chat**, pin, move to folder and the drag handle appear when you point at a card or tab into it. **Chat** starts a new conversation with the agent and opens it.

The **Teams** page now reads **Agents › Teams**, and its side panel offers **New team** and **Import team** instead of the agent actions. Teams are cards in the same grid. Each card shows the members as a row of avatars: the lead comes first and wears a star, and **+N** stands for the rest. Select the member count to list everyone by name. **Start conversation** is the card's main button. **Export**, **Edit** and **Delete** are in the **⋯** menu, and **Delete** asks you to confirm. A project with no teams explains what a team is and offers **New team**. See [Agents](/menus/agents) and [Saved teams of agents](/how-tos/agents-pipelines/agent-teams#manage-teams).
