## The admin console in six labelled areas

The admin console's sidebar now lists its pages in six labelled areas: People and access, Projects, Models and spend, Agents and governance, Security, and Platform. Each person sees only the areas and items their administration roles open, so a security auditor sees Security › Audit Trail and nothing else. Tasks and Schedules & Tasks are now one item, Schedules and tasks, with a tab for each page. Typing the address of a page your roles do not open shows which roles open it and whom to ask, and never names an administrator. A new deployment has the areas on; an upgraded deployment keeps the earlier navigation until an administrator turns on **Admin › Features › Administration console › Six labelled areas**. See [Six labelled areas](/admin/overview#six-labelled-areas).
