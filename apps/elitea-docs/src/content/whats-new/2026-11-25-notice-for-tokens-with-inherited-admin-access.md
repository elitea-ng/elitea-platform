## A notice for tokens with inherited administration access

Once the end date of inherited administration access is announced, each owner of a personal access token marked **All your administration permissions** gets one e-mail that lists those tokens and the date their administration access ends. That access then ends on the announced date or 30 days after the e-mail was delivered, whichever is later, so nobody loses it without notice. The token list shows the date on each such token. Until the date is announced, nothing is sent. See [Create a personal access token](/getting-started/create-personal-access-token).
