## Models and spend has a page for each LLM gateway control

The LLM gateway controls are no longer tabs of **Configuration › LLM Proxy**. Each one is a page of the **Models and spend** area: **Providers and models**, **Models and pricing**, **Tool prices**, **Usage** (with the gateway **Status** as a tab), **Logs**, **LLM Governance** and **Budgets and alerts** (with **Budget alerts** as a tab). A person who manages models and spend, such as an LLM manager, finds all of it in one area and never needs Configuration. Old bookmarks of the LLM Proxy section open the matching page. See [LLM proxy](/admin/llm-proxy#where-the-pages-were).
