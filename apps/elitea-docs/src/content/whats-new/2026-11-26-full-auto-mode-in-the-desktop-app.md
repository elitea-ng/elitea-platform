## Full auto for long runs you do not watch

A desktop Local work thread's mode menu now offers **Full auto** above Bypass. The agent answers every approval for you, including the questions asked after it read outside content in a trusted folder, so a long run does not stop to wait. Recursive deletes outside the folder, credential stores, shell profiles and turning a protection off are still asked, or declined when nobody is there. Turning it on asks for confirmation every time, it applies to that thread only, and a red line says it is on. Every answer is recorded, and the web and mobile app mark the answer **Ran in Full auto**. Your organization can forbid it. See [Full auto](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-full-auto).
