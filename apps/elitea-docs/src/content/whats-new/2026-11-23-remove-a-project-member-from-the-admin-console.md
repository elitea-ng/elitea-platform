## Manage the members of any project from the admin console

Project administrators, admins and super admins can now manage the members of any project from the admin **Projects** page without being a member themselves. **Manage project member** gains **Remove from project**: type the address of someone already in the project and remove them; their account stays. A change made in a project you are not a member of is marked as a central override in the audit trail. You still cannot give yourself permissions you do not hold this way: the refusal tells you to ask a member of the project who manages its members, or an operator of the deployment.
