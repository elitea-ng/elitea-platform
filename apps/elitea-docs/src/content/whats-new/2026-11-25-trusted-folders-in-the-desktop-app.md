## Trust a folder to run its commands without a sandbox

In the desktop app, a folder's **⋯** menu now offers **Trust this folder…**. After a warning that says what the folder's commands could then read, change and reach, and your explicit confirmation, its commands run as your own processes: your environment and `PATH`, your command-line logins and the network. Approvals, your rules and your organization's policy still apply, and after the agent reads outside content in a turn, commands are asked again. A **No sandbox** badge shows on the folder, the thread title and the composer; **Trust paused** shows when your organization's policy or a changed settings file holds it. Revoke from the badge, the folder menu or the new **Settings ▸ Trusted folders**. On Windows, a command refused for want of a sandbox offers the same. See [Run a folder's commands without a sandbox](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-trusted-folders).
