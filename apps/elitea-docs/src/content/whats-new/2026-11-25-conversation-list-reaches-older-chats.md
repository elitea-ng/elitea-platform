## The conversation list reaches older chats after deletions elsewhere

When you deleted, archived or moved conversations in another tab or on another device, scrolling the chat list could stop short: the older conversations below never loaded until you reloaded the page. The web and desktop list now notices that its place in a date group or folder has shifted and reads that group again from the top, so the older conversations load and the ones that left the group disappear. The mobile Chats list does the same and refreshes its pages.
