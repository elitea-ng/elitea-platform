## Administration scopes on personal access tokens

When you create a personal access token in the web app, you can give it exactly the administration permissions it needs. The form lists only the permissions you hold, and never offers the global administrator's own permission, identity trust or break-glass. Choosing any needs a recent sign-in: the form takes you through it and keeps what you entered. The token list shows each token's administration access, and marks older tokens that inherit all of your permissions. See [Create a personal access token](/getting-started/create-personal-access-token#administration-access) and [Administration scopes on a token](/admin/authentication#administration-scopes-on-a-token).
