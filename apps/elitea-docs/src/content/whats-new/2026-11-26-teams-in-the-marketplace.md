## Teams in the Marketplace

A saved team can now be published to the Marketplace and hired like an agent. **Publish to Marketplace** on a team row lists it once every member agent is itself published; the Marketplace finds it under the new **Teams** kind. **Hire** on a team listing installs or forks every member, with the connections each needs, and saves the team in your project pinned to the hired agents. With **Install**, a member your project already installed from the same version is reused. See [Teams](/menus/marketplace#teams).
