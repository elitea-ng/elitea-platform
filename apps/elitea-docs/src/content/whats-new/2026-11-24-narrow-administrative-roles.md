## Assign several administration roles from the Users page

The Users page now lets you give a person several administration roles at once, built-in or custom, from one picker per person. Built-in roles are shown by their display name, such as *LLM manager*, *Security auditor* or *Project administrator (all projects)*, with a line on what each one is for; the person holds the permissions of every role they have. A person with a narrow role now finds the pages they can use in the console's navigation: an LLM manager sees Configuration, LLM Governance, Budgets and Projects. See [Several administration roles per person](/admin/users-and-roles#several-administration-roles-per-person) and [What a narrow role sees](/admin/overview#what-a-narrow-role-sees).
