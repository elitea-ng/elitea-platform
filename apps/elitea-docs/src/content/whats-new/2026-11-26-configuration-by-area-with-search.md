## Configuration is grouped by area and has a search

The **Configuration** page of the admin console groups its sections under the console area each belongs to, and lists only the sections your roles can open. A search box above the list finds a section by its title or by the label of any of its fields. A section you can view but not change opens as **View only**, with its save disabled. See [Features and configuration](/admin/features-and-configuration#configuration).
