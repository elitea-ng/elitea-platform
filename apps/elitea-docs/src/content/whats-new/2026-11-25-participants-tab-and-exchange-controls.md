## A Participants tab, @ agents and exchange controls in desktop work threads

In the desktop app, a **Local work** thread with several agents now has a **Participants** tab in the inspector: each agent shows whether it runs **Local** or in the **Cloud**, which one is the **Lead**, and a menu to make or clear the lead, address it, move it here or to the cloud, open it or remove it. Typing **@** in the composer lists the thread's agents before the folder's files, and **To: Name** above the message box shows who will answer. While several agents answer one message in turn, a strip shows **Now** and **Next**, with **Stop exchange** and a skip button on each waiting agent. See [The Participants tab](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-multi-agent#the-participants-tab) and [Watch and step in](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-multi-agent#watch-and-step-in).
