## Marketplace feedback: reviews, workforce reports and signals

A listing page now has a **Feedback** section. People whose project hired the listing can post one review per listing (1 to 5 and optional text) and edit it. The section shows the rating and its distribution per version, **signals** beside it (active hires, 30-day retention, dismissal rate, upgrade adoption, computed daily), and the newest reviews and **workforce reports**, with a version filter. A review shows its author's name; a project name is never shown. The publisher can reply once to an item and flag it, and an administrator can hide it, with an audit event. The admin published agents view now fills its project counter. The mobile agent sheet in Discover shows the rating and feedback, read-only. **Run the migrations** (shared `0252`) and upgrade Main and the clients together. See [Marketplace](/menus/marketplace#feedback).
