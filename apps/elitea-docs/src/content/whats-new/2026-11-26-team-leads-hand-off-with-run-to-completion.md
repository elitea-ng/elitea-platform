## A team's lead hands off to its members on its own

In a desktop **Local work** thread run as a team, the lead now hands work to the team's members by itself, also when **Run to completion** is on (it is on by default). Before, a lead that ran to completion had no way to hand off: it asked you to add its members and address them by hand. The lead's instructions now name the agents already in the thread and where each runs, and a hand-off ends the run: the member it handed to answers next, on this computer with the folder. See [Agents hand off to each other](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-multi-agent#agents-hand-off-to-each-other).
