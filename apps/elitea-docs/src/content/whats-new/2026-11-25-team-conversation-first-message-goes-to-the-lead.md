## A team conversation's first message goes to its lead

In a conversation started from a team with a lead, the composer named the lead ("To: Name (lead)"), but a first message that named no agent was refused with "This conversation has several agents. Pick the agent this message is for." The lead now answers it, as the composer says, on the web and in the desktop app (the mobile app already sent it to the lead). A conversation whose agents have no lead still asks you to pick one.
