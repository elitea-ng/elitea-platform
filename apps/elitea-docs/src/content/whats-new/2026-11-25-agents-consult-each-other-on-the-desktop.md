## Agents consult each other on the desktop

When an agent of a desktop team thread calls another agent of the thread as a nested agent, the call now runs on your computer, in the thread's folder, under the thread's approvals. The question and the answer show in the thread as side messages between the two agents, and they stay there when you reopen the thread on the web or on mobile. See [Agents consult each other](/how-tos/chat-conversations/desktop-multi-agent#agents-consult-each-other).
