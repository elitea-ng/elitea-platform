**Agent Zefir Support**

Agent Zefir is a mobile app for your organization's AI-agent workspace. Most
questions about agents, access and data are answered by your workspace
administrator; questions about the app itself come to us.

### Getting started

1. Get your workspace address from your administrator (for example
   `ai.example.com`), or a QR code or `agentzefir://` link.
2. Open Agent Zefir, enter or scan the address, and check the workspace name on
   the confirmation screen.
3. Sign in with your organization's usual account. The sign-in page opens in a
   secure system sheet; the app never sees your password.
4. Turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock when asked, if your device
   has it.

### Common questions

**"This workspace is not supported" or the sign-in button is missing.**
The workspace must be an Elitea-compatible server that allows the Agent Zefir
app. Ask your administrator to enable native clients for `ca.zefir.agent`.

**"Update required."** The workspace needs a newer version of the app. Update
it from the App Store or Google Play.

**I lost my phone.** Ask your administrator, or use your workspace's "My
devices" page, to sign the device out. The app deletes its data from the phone
the next time it reaches the workspace.

**How do I remove everything from this phone?** Settings, **Log out & wipe**.
Uninstalling the app also removes it.

**Can I use more than one workspace?** Yes. Open the workspace switcher from
the home screen and add another.

**Is my dictation sent anywhere?** No. Speech is turned into text on the
device.

**The app shows old content or "Offline".** It is showing the encrypted
offline copy because the workspace cannot be reached. Messages you write are
sent when the connection returns.
