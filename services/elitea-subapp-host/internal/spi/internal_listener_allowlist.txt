# elitea-subapp-host's internal listeners: the reviewed allowlist (ADR-0059
# decision 7, "Internal paths"; story R1.8). internal_listener_surface_test.go
# compares it with the methods the host's gRPC listener serves and with the
# listener and registration sites of the module's source, both ways. The
# format and the meaning of each field are in internal_listener_checks_test.go.
#
# The host writes no administration data (provider, policy, identity or role
# data): it reads policy from PostgreSQL or receives it from elitea-main. An
# "admin" line here would have to be listed in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md.

# --- gRPC listener (<PREFIX>PLATFORM_GRPC_ADDR) ---------------------------------
# One listener, mTLS with a client certificate required; every call is
# authorised by the verified certificate against <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS
# (elitea-main) before any handler runs. Main calls it after its own decision.
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/Describe | read | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/Health | read | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/Slots | read | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/Invoke | write | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/GetInvocation | read | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.ProviderService/Cancel | write | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
# Remove what the application holds for a project or a toolkit Main deleted.
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.PlatformOperations/DeleteProject | write | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
grpc | platform | /elitea.subapp.v1.PlatformOperations/DeleteToolkit | write | workload | <PREFIX>PLATFORM_CLIENTS (elitea-main)
