# MDM feedback from the mobile app (#1717, ADR-0052 W6.1)

On a managed device the mobile app now reports its state back to the MDM. On
iOS that is managed app feedback (`com.apple.feedback.managed`); on Android it
is keyed app states.

- **Who is affected:** administrators who configure the app through an MDM or
  EMM, and builders of the Android app.
- **What to do:** nothing. To see the states, use your MDM's app feedback view
  (Intune, Workspace ONE, Test DPC). Docs: "Managed devices (MDM)" (Admin),
  "What the app reports back".
- **Behaviour:** the app reports four keys (`managed_config`, `connection`,
  `sign_in`, `policy`) with fixed codes, and never user content. It reports
  nothing without a managed configuration. The Android app gains the
  dependency `androidx.enterprise:enterprise-feedback:1.1.0`, added to
  `app/build.gradle` by `plugins/withManagedConfiguration.js`.
