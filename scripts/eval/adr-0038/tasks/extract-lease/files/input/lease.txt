RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on December 12, 2026 between
HARBOR VIEW PROPERTIES LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Landlord"),
and MARIA ELENA RUIZ and KWAME MENSAH, jointly and severally ("Tenant").
The Premises are managed on Landlord's behalf by Cascade Property Management,
Inc. ("Agent"). Agent is not a party to this Lease.

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the residential unit located at
   2210 Westlake Avenue N, Unit 504, Seattle, WA 98109 (the "Premises"),
   together with one (1) assigned parking space, No. 14, in the garage.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on the first (1st) day of
   February, 2027 (the "Commencement Date") and shall continue for a period
   of eighteen (18) months, ending at 11:59 p.m. on the last day of the
   eighteenth month.

3. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Two Thousand Four Hundred Fifty
   Dollars ($2,450.00), payable in advance on the first (1st) day of each
   calendar month, to Agent by ACH transfer. Rent for any partial month shall
   be prorated on a daily basis.

4. RENT ADJUSTMENT. Monthly rent shall increase by three and one-half percent
   (3.5%) on each anniversary of the Commencement Date during the term.

5. LATE CHARGE. If rent is not received within five (5) days after the due
   date, Tenant shall pay a late charge of Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00).
   Returned payments incur an additional fee of $35.00.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with
   Agent the sum of Four Thousand Nine Hundred Dollars ($4,900.00) as a
   security deposit. The deposit is not a limit on Tenant's liability and may
   not be applied by Tenant to the last month's rent.

7. UTILITIES. Landlord shall pay for water, sewer and trash collection.
   Tenant shall arrange and pay for electricity, natural gas (which also
   supplies the unit's heating), internet and any cable or streaming
   services.

8. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence
   for Tenant and no more than one (1) additional occupant approved in writing.

9. PETS. No animals, birds or pets of any kind shall be kept on the Premises,
   except service or assistance animals as required by law.

10. TERMINATION AND NOTICE. Either party may terminate this Lease at the end
    of the term by giving written notice of not less than eight (8) weeks
    before the end of the term. If no notice is given, this Lease converts to
    a month-to-month tenancy on the same terms.

11. MAINTENANCE. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and promptly report any
    defects to Agent. Landlord is responsible for repairs to structural
    elements, plumbing, heating and electrical systems not caused by Tenant.

12. ENTRY. Landlord or Agent may enter the Premises with at least two (2)
    days' written notice, or without notice in an emergency.

13. GOVERNING LAW. This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of
    Washington, including the Residential Landlord-Tenant Act, RCW 59.18.
