# Senior Data Engineer (part-time) — Fernhill Energy

**Location:** Manchester, United Kingdom
**Team:** Data Platform
**Posted:** 2 December 2026

## About us

Fernhill Energy supplies renewable electricity to 400,000 homes across the
North of England. Our Data Platform team owns the pipelines that turn smart
meter readings into bills, forecasts and the half-hourly settlement files we
send to the market operator. Our stack also includes Snowflake, Looker and a
fair amount of legacy SSIS that we are retiring.

## The role

You will report to the Head of Data Platform and work alongside four other
engineers. This is a part-time position: 30 hours per week (0.8 FTE),
spread over four days. It is a permanent contract.

We work hybrid: the team is together in our Manchester office on Tuesdays
and Thursdays, and the rest of your working days are from home. Occasional
travel to our Leeds control room (two or three times a year) is expected.

## What you'll do

- Build and operate batch and streaming pipelines for meter data
- Own data quality checks for the settlement process
- Mentor two junior engineers
- Take part in a light on-call rota (one week in six, business hours only)

## What you'll need

At least four years of professional data engineering experience, including
time spent running pipelines in production.

Required skills:

- Python
- PostgreSQL
- Airflow
- dbt

Nice to have:

- Kubernetes
- Terraform

## Pay and benefits

Salary: £52,000 – £64,000 per year, which is the pro-rated pay for 30 hours
(full-time equivalent £65,000 – £80,000). For context, recent industry surveys
put full-time senior data engineers in the North West at £75k – £95k; we aim
to be competitive on total package rather than base alone.

- 8% employer pension contribution
- 27 days holiday plus bank holidays (pro-rated)
- £1,000 annual learning budget (not pro-rated)
- Cycle-to-work and EV salary sacrifice schemes

## Right to work

We are unable to sponsor visas for this role. Applicants must already have
the right to work in the UK.

## How to apply

Send your CV and a short note about a pipeline you are proud of to
careers@fernhill-energy.example. Applications close on 31 January 2027.

Interviews: a 30-minute call with the hiring manager, then a 90-minute
technical session (no take-home task).
