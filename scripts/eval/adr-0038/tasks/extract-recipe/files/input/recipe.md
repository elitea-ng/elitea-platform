# Lemon Blueberry Loaf (the one my neighbours keep asking for)

Posted by Ruth in *Weekend Baking* · 2,418 saves

My grandmother made this with 2 cups of flour and a whole stick of margarine,
but over the years I've lightened it up. It keeps for three days wrapped in
foil, and it freezes well (slice it first).

**Serves:** 4 (two generous slices each)
**Prep:** 20 mins · **Cook:** 1 hr 5 mins · **Cooling:** 15 mins in the tin
**Oven:** 180°C / 160°C fan / 350°F / gas mark 4

---

## Ingredients

### For the loaf

- 1 1/2 cups (190 g) plain flour
- 3/4 cup (150 g) caster sugar
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 1 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
- 4 oz (115 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 2/3 cup fresh blueberries, tossed in a little of the flour

### For the topping

- 2 tbsp demerara sugar
- zest of 1 lemon (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oven and line a 2 lb (900 g) loaf tin with baking paper.
2. Whisk the flour, salt and baking powder together in a bowl.
3. In a second bowl beat the sugar and eggs for 2 minutes until pale, then
   whisk in the milk and the melted butter.
4. Fold the wet mixture into the dry until just combined. A few streaks of
   flour are fine.
5. Gently fold in the blueberries.
6. Scrape into the tin, then scatter the demerara sugar and lemon zest on top.
7. Bake for 1 hr 5 mins, until a skewer comes out clean. If the top browns too
   fast after 40 minutes, cover loosely with foil.
8. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then lift out onto a rack.

## Notes

- Frozen blueberries work; don't thaw them, and add 5 minutes to the bake.
- For a glaze, mix 100 g icing sugar with 2 tbsp lemon juice once the loaf is
  completely cold (not included in the times above).
